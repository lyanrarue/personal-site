## Background

I absolutely love coffee—especially pour-over. A few years ago, a coworker introduced me to it, and I’ve been down the rabbit hole ever since.

I’ve grown particularly fond of light-roast washed Ethiopian and Kenyan coffees, and I usually focus on high-clarity brewing.

Below is a running set of notes on the roasters I’ve enjoyed and the equipment I use regularly.

## Roasters

- [SEY](https://www.seycoffee.com/)
